Calibrated Golf - App Repair

Both repair options keep your saved settings, scores, rounds and Buddies identity. Connect to the internet and keep this page open while repairing.

1. Repair startup

Try this first if the app will not open. It updates the startup component and keeps the cached app.

2. Clear cached app

This keeps the original repair option for a stuck or outdated app. It removes the stored app pages and resets the startup component, then opens a fresh copy. The app will need to download again.

Clear the cached app?

The stored app pages will be removed and downloaded again. Your saved settings, scores, rounds and Buddies identity will be kept.
